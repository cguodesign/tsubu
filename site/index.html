One Japanese sentence, read along grain by grain.一句日语，一粒一粒地跟着读。

tsubu is a small, dependency-free browser library that draws exactly one sentence for read-along and language learning: a dotted line under each segment, furigana that switch on and off without moving a character, and a highlight that follows the voice as a pure function of time.

tsubu 是一个很小、零依赖的浏览器库，只画一句话，用于跟读和语言学习：每段一条点状下划线，注音开关时任何一个字都不移动，高亮作为时间的纯函数跟着声音走。

Every frame is drawn from the time alone, by renderAt(t): the same page plays here and renders to video frame by frame. The sentence is drawn by tsubu with the appear preset; the field, the ink grain and the large reading follow the state tsubu reports for that time. The times are set to a beat, not measured from a recording.

每一帧都只由时间决定，由 renderAt(t) 画出：同一个页面既在这里播放，也能逐帧渲染成视频。句子由 tsubu 用 appear 预设绘制；底色、墨色米粒和大字读音都跟着 tsubu 在那个时刻报告的状态走。时间是按节拍定的，不是从录音里量的。

透Under the loupe放大镜下

Move over the sentence to read its furigana, with each segment's box and its time. On a phone, tap a word; with the keyboard, focus the sentence and use the arrow keys.

把指针移到句子上，就能看到注音、每一段的框和它的时间。手机上点一个词；用键盘时，先聚焦句子，再按方向键。

The sentence is drawn twice from the same HTML, once with furigana off and once with them on. The two layers line up exactly because switching furigana in tsubu moves nothing.

同一段 HTML 画了两遍，一遍不带注音，一遍带。两层完全对齐，因为在 tsubu 里开关注音什么都不会动。

粒What it does它做什么

  • One grain per segment一段一粒The sentence arrives already cut into segments (for Japanese, bunsetsu). Each gets its own dotted underline; a word with a reading is never split.句子传进来时已经切好了段（日语里就是文节）。每段有自己的点状下划线；带读音的词不会被拆开。
  • Furigana that move nothing开关注音，字不动Readings are <ruby> and switch on and off without moving a character, which a test measures in a real browser.读音是 <ruby>，开关时任何一个字都不移动；有测试在真实浏览器里量过。
  • A frame is a function of t每一帧都是 t 的函数stateAt(t) and apply(root, t) are pure. No timers, no transitions: live playback and frame-by-frame capture draw the same thing.stateAt(t) 和 apply(root, t) 是纯函数。没有计时器、没有过渡：实时播放和逐帧截图画出来完全一样。
  • Measured colours实测的颜色Themes for a light page, a dark page and text over video, colours per speaker, and checkContrast, which measures every state against WCAG-based thresholds.浅色页、深色页和叠在视频上的三套主题，按说话人上色，以及按 WCAG 阈值测量每个状态的 checkContrast。
  • Japanese lines日语的行Lines break only between segments and by Japanese line-breaking rules, stay close in length, take their size from the container, and are paged by t. Vertical writing too.只在段与段之间、按禁则断行，各行长度接近，字号随容器变化，超长时按 t 翻页。也支持竖排。
  • Motion that shows the reading表现朗读的动效Presets appear, mask, blur and underline-draw, each drawn from the state at t, and revealing on the learner's own events.预设 appear、mask、blur、underline-draw，都由 t 时刻的状态画出；也能按学习者的操作揭示。

What tsubu does not do: split text into segments, align audio, estimate times, play audio, draw a speech bubble or lay out more than one sentence. Those belong to the caller.

tsubu 不做的事：切分文本、对齐音频、估算时间、播放音频、画对话气泡、排多句话。这些由调用方负责。
