tsubu粒

A small library that draws one Japanese sentence for reading along: a segment at a time, furigana that switch on and off without moving a character, and a highlight that follows the voice.

動In time

tsubu
0.00 s

Every frame is drawn from the time alone, by renderAt(t): the same page plays here and renders to video frame by frame. The sentence is drawn by tsubu with the appear preset; the field, the ink grain and the large reading follow the state tsubu reports for that time. The times are set to a beat, not measured from a recording.

透Under the loupe

Move over the sentence to read its furigana, with each segment's box and its time. On a phone, tap a word; with the keyboard, focus the sentence and use the arrow keys.

The sentence is drawn twice from the same HTML, once with furigana off and once with them on. The two layers line up exactly because switching furigana in tsubu moves nothing.
