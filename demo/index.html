tsubu demo

One sentence per card, drawn by Tsubu.sentence() and updated with apply(root, t). Drag the time to move the highlight; untick the box to hide the furigana, or pick another theme, and see that nothing moves. Under each card, Tsubu.checkContrast() reports the lowest contrast it measured in any state; "none" shows the unthemed defaults, which are not a preset and do not pass. The sentences were written for this page and their times are invented, not measured. The address accepts ?t=seconds&ruby=off&theme=dark.
