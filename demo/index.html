tsubu demo

One sentence per card, drawn by Tsubu.sentence() and updated with apply(root, t). Drag the time to move the highlight; untick the box to hide the furigana, or pick another theme, and see that nothing moves. Under each card, Tsubu.checkContrast() reports the lowest contrast it measured in any state; "none" shows the unthemed defaults, which are not a preset and do not pass. The sentences were written for this page and their times are invented, not measured. The motion card hides what has not been heard yet; click a segment to reveal it and see its time, where a player would seek to. お茶を carries the tag new-word, which this page draws with a solid underline. The vertical card is the same library with writing: "vertical". The last card is a fluid frame: drag its width to see the size follow it, and set a maximum number of lines to see the sentence paged by t. The address accepts ?t=seconds&ruby=off&theme=dark&frame=320&lines=2&motion=mask.
